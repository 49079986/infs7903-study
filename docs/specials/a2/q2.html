解答、逐行代码与实测依据 / Solution, line guide and evidence

The query returns count 116 and sum 86,382.67. The aggregate estimates and returns one result row, with cost 410.92..410.93 and execution time 0.292 ms. Its child Bitmap Index Scan finds 116 matching entries, versus 132 estimated, and Bitmap Heap Scan visits 79 exact heap blocks before computing the aggregate.

event_type 等值固定第一列，3 月上下界限定第二列，形成很短的索引范围。只匹配约 0.059% 的事件，远比全部 Collect 更有选择性。amount 不在索引中，所以仍需要 heap 访问。

Incident equality fixes the leading key, and the lower and upper March timestamp bounds restrict the second key to a short contiguous range within Incident entries. These 116 events are about 0.059 percent of the table, so the bitmap index path avoids most rows and pages. The plan needs heap access for amount, which is not stored in the index. This is much more selective than the all-time Collect query in Q2.1 part 2.

（1分）提取不区分大小写的邮箱域，按成员数降序统计。

(1 marks) Extract case-insensitive provider domains and count members per domain, most popular first.

解答、逐行代码与实测依据 / Solution, line guide and evidence

LOWER(SPLIT_PART(email, '@', 2)) 先取域名，再统一小写，避免把 Bigpond.com 与 bigpond.com 分成两组。12 个域名的总数是 24,000；gmail.com 以 8,164 人最多，exemail.com.au 为 248 人。人数相同时按域名排序，便于复查。

LOWER(SPLIT_PART(email, '@', 2)) extracts the part after @ and normalizes its case before grouping. The query reports 12 domains accounting for all 24,000 members, with gmail.com first at 8,164 members. exemail.com.au has 248 members. The secondary alphabetical ordering makes equal counts reproducible.

A2_2.2.1.sql
-- INFS7903 Assignment 2 | Public teaching edition
SELECT LOWER(SPLIT_PART(email, '@', 2)) AS provider_domain,
       COUNT(*) AS member_count
FROM MEMBERS
GROUP BY LOWER(SPLIT_PART(email, '@', 2))
ORDER BY member_count DESC, provider_domain;

中文题意 / Task in Chinese

（1分）解释完整email唯一索引为何不能直接按域条件定位。

(1 marks) Explain why the existing UNIQUE email index cannot directly answer provider-domain predicates.

解答、逐行代码与实测依据 / Solution, line guide and evidence

email 的 UNIQUE B-tree 按完整邮箱从第一个字符开始排序，同域名地址可能散落在不同用户名后面，因此不能按规范化域名直接定位或提供域名分组顺序。即使完整扫描 email 索引后再提取域名，也仍要检查所有成员。

The UNIQUE B-tree on email orders complete addresses from their first character, so members sharing a domain can be scattered across many different address prefixes. It cannot provide a selective lookup or grouping order on the normalized domain expression; although a full index-only scan could still read every email and compute the domain, it would still have to examine the whole population.
