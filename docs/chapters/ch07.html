INFS7903 · CH07

关系代数与查询表达Relational algebra and query formulation

学习目标与运算闭包Learning objectives and relational closure

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 3-11（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

目标是把自然语言查询改写成关系代数（relational algebra，RA），逐步解释每个中间关系的模式与含义，并检查等价变换是否丢失必要信息。前置是集合、布尔逻辑、键与SQL连接。关系运算的输入与输出都是关系，所以一个运算的结果可以继续成为下一个输入，这就是运算闭包性质。

The goal is to translate natural-language queries into relational algebra (RA), explain each intermediate relation’s schema and meaning, and check whether transformations lose needed information. Prerequisites are sets, Boolean logic, keys and SQL joins. Relational operators take relations and return relations, so one result becomes the next input: the closure property.

SQL通常声明“要什么”，RA表达式给出高层的操作组合，但仍未指定磁盘如何扫描、连接用哪种物理算法。第8章把逻辑运算变成具体实现。经典RA采用集合语义，投影去重；SQL默认袋语义（bag semantics）可保留重复，比较SQL与RA时须显式说明这一点。

SQL usually declares what is wanted, while RA specifies a high-level composition of operations. It still does not specify disk scans or a physical join algorithm; Chapter 8 supplies those implementations. Classical RA has set semantics and projection removes duplicates. SQL normally has bag semantics and can retain duplicates, so state this difference when comparing them.

选择、投影与重命名Selection, projection and renaming

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 12-22（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

选择（selection）σ条件(R)保留满足条件的行，列不变；投影（projection）π属性(R)保留指定列并消除重复，行数不会增加。RA的σ对应SQL的WHERE，而不是SQL SELECT关键字；SQL SELECT列列表更接近π。重命名（rename）ρ给关系或属性新名，尤其用于自连接与集合兼容。

Selection σcondition (R) retains qualifying rows without changing columns. Projection πattributes (R) retains columns and removes duplicates, so it cannot increase row count. RA selection corresponds to SQL WHERE, not the SQL SELECT keyword; the SQL SELECT list is closer to projection. Rename ρ gives a relation or attributes new names, especially for self-joins and set compatibility.

T ← σDno=5 ∧ Salary>30000(EMPLOYEE)
RESULT ← πFname,Lname(T)

例如员工John与Joyce在部门5，Alice在部门4，选择部门5后只剩前两人；若仅投影Dno，两行都变成(5)，集合投影只保留一个。把πDno提前到σSalary>30000之前会丢掉Salary，后面条件无法计算。每次操作后写出剩余属性，是发现这类错误最有效的方法。

Suppose John and Joyce work in department 5 and Alice in department 4. Selecting department 5 retains the first two. Projecting only Dno maps both to (5), leaving one tuple under set projection. Moving πDno before σSalary>30000 removes Salary and makes the predicate impossible to evaluate. Writing each intermediate schema is an effective way to detect this mistake.

集合操作、兼容与布尔逻辑Set operations, compatibility and Boolean logic

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 24-39（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

并（union）R∪S是任一集合中的行，交（intersection）R∩S是两者都有，差（difference）R−S只在R中。三者要求并相容（union compatibility）：属性数量相同，对应域相容，必要时先重命名。员工项目表和家属表不能直接并，但各自投影ESSN后可以并或交。

Union R∪S contains tuples in either relation, intersection R∩S those in both, and difference R−S those only in R. They require union compatibility: equal arity and compatible corresponding domains, with renaming when needed. WORKS_ON and DEPENDENT cannot be directly union ed, but their ESSN projections can be union ed or intersected.

工作或有家属 / works or has dependants:　πESSN(WORKS_ON) ∪ πESSN(DEPENDENT)
两者都有 / both:　πESSN(WORKS_ON) ∩ πESSN(DEPENDENT)
有家属但无工作 / dependants but no project:　πESSN(DEPENDENT) − πESSN(WORKS_ON)

并、交可交换且可结合，差通常不可交换也不可结合。布尔AND是同时满足，OR是至少一个满足；不能把OR筛选拆成串联σ，因为串联表示AND。De Morgan规则¬(P∧Q)=¬P∨¬Q，¬(P∨Q)=¬P∧¬Q。对“既不在Brisbane，也不归部门4”的项目，正确条件是Plocation≠Brisbane AND Dnum≠4。

Union and intersection are commutative and associative; difference generally is neither. Boolean AND requires both conditions and OR at least one. A disjunction cannot become successive selections because successive selections mean AND. De Morgan’s laws are ¬(P∧Q)=¬P∨¬Q and ¬(P∨Q)=¬P∧¬Q. Projects neither located in Brisbane nor controlled by department 4 require Plocation≠Brisbane AND Dnum≠4.

乘积、连接与自连接Products, joins and self-joins

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 40-55（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

笛卡尔积（Cartesian product）R×S生成所有配对，若各r、s行则有rs行，列数相加。θ连接（theta join）R⋈θS相当于σθ(R×S)，θ可以是=、<等比较；等值连接（equi-join）只使用相等条件。自然连接（natural join）自动在全部同名列相等处匹配，并只留一份公共列。若没有同名列，自然连接变成乘积，不会猜到Dno应该等于Dnumber。

The Cartesian product R×S forms every pair: r and s input rows yield rs rows and the combined arity. A theta join R⋈θS equals σθ(R×S), with comparisons such as = or <; an equi-join uses equality only. A natural join matches on all identically named columns and keeps one copy of each common column. With no shared names it becomes a product; it does not infer that Dno should match Dnumber.

自连接（self-join）要区分“员工角色”和“经理角色”。先把EMPLOYEE重命名为E与M，然后按E.SuperSSN=M.SSN连接。若直接自然连接同一个模式，所有同名列都会被限制相等，结果不再是经理关系。查询部门经理要按D.MgrSSN=E.SSN，而不是E.Dno=D.Dnumber，后者得到部门所有员工。

A self-join distinguishes employee and manager roles. Rename EMPLOYEE as E and M, then join on E.SuperSSN=M.SSN. Naturally joining identical schemas constrains every shared column to equality and does not express supervision. To find department managers, join D.MgrSSN=E.SSN; E. Dno=D.Dnumber retrieves all departmental employees instead.

πE.Fname,E.Lname,M.Fname,M.Lname(ρE(EMPLOYEE) ⋈E.SuperSSN=M.SSN ρM(EMPLOYEE))

内连接（inner join）丢弃没有匹配的行。左外连接（left outer join）保留左侧全部行，右侧缺失属性补NULL；右外连接反向保留。想保留没有经理的员工，可把员工放左侧做左外连接；不能在后面WHERE M.Salary>某值而又期待无经理行保留，因为NULL比较会把它们滤掉。需要区分ON中的匹配限制与WHERE的最终筛选。

An inner join discards unmatched rows. A left outer join retains every left row, filling missing right attributes with NULL; a right outer join reverses the retained side. To retain employees without managers, place employees on the left of a left join. A later WHERE M. Salary>some value removes the NULL-extended rows, so distinguish ON match conditions from final WHERE filtering.

除法与“全部”的反例法Division and the counterexample interpretation of every

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 56-63（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

除法（division）R(Y,X)÷S(X)返回这样的Y：对S中的每一个X，都有(Y,X)出现在R。它检验集合包含，不是简单相等，也允许候选人多做项目。一个完整例子：R={(Ana,P1),(Ana,P2),(Bo,P1)}，S={P1,P2}，结果只有Ana，因为Bo缺(P2)。

Division R(Y, X)÷S(X) returns Y values paired in R with every X in S. It tests containment rather than exact equality and permits extra projects. For R={(Ana, P1),(Ana, P2),(Bo, P1)} and S={P1, P2}, only Ana qualifies because Bo lacks P2.

T₁ ← πY(R)
Missing ← (T₁ × S) − R
Bad ← πY(Missing)
Result ← T₁ − Bad

推导顺序是先列候选人，再列每个候选人应该具备的全部组合，减去已做到的组合，剩下的就是反例。投影反例中的Y得到失败者，最后从候选人集合删掉失败者。这个表达式说明{σ,π,∪,−,×}足以构成核心RA运算；连接可由乘积加选择表达，交可由R−(R−S)表达，通常还需用ρ管理名字。

The construction first lists candidates, then every candidate–requirement pair, subtracts pairs actually present and identifies counterexamples. Project their Y values to obtain failures, then remove them from the candidates. This explains the expressive completeness of the core operators {σ,π,∪,−,×}: joins use product plus selection, and intersection is R−(R−S), with ρ normally available to manage names.

交互：找出缺失组合Interactive: identify missing pairs

边界是候选人来源。标准上式从πY(R)取候选人，若要求集合S为空，返回πY(R)。如果业务候选人是所有EMPLOYEE，包括从没出现在WORKS_ON中的人，应把T₁定义成完整员工集合，然后按缺失配对法判断。这样才能与第2章从EMPLOYEE出发的双重NOT EXISTS在空目标时对应。

The candidate source is a boundary condition. The standard construction takes candidates from πY(R), so an empty S returns πY(R). If business candidates include every EMPLOYEE, even those absent from WORKS_ON, define T₁ as the complete employee set before testing missing pairs. That matches Chapter 2’s double NOT EXISTS rooted in EMPLOYEE when the required set is empty.

完整查询的构造与检查Constructing and checking a complete query

来源 / Source: Lecture/Week07-RA(1).pdf · PDF p. 65-71（原资料留在本地版） · 本地逐页图文 / Local pages

查“Research部门员工姓名和地址”：先σDname=Research(DEPARTMENT)，按Dnumber=Dno连EMPLOYEE，再πFname,Lname,Address。查“做部门5全部项目”：先投影部门5的PNUMBER并重命名PNO，作为除数；从WORKS_ON取ESSN,PNO为被除数；得到员工键后再连接姓名。先得到键再附加显示属性，能避免姓名重复和集合不相容问题。

For names and addresses of Research employees, select Dname=Research from DEPARTMENT, join EMPLOYEE on Dnumber=Dno and project names and address. For employees on every department-5 project, project the selected PNUMBER values and rename them PNO as the divisor; use WORKS_ON’s ESSN, PNO as the dividend. Join the resulting employee keys back to names. Deriving keys before display attributes avoids duplicate-name and compatibility problems.

解题检查四项：每个条件引用的列是否仍存在；每条连接是否对应真实关系；集合操作两侧是否相容；结果一行的含义是否符合题意。课程pp13–71共有27个编号例题，已在课堂题库全部登记并提供表达式；Tutorial 7另按原题登记，包括标为skip的三道聚合题，补充解法会明确标注扩展运算。

Check four things: whether each predicate’s columns remain available, whether each join follows an intended relationship, whether set operands are compatible and whether output rows represent the required unit. The 27 numbered lecture examples on pp 13–71 are registered with expressions in the classroom bank. 总结与自测Summary and self-check

RA是贯穿SQL语义和优化的共同语言。选择减行、投影减列与重复，连接组合相关事实，差与除处理否定和全称要求。写长式子前先写中间关系的自然语言含义，再核对模式，通常比直接拼符号更容易正确。

RA connects SQL semantics with optimisation. Selection reduces rows, projection reduces columns and duplicates, joins combine related facts, and difference and division express negation and universal requirements. Explain intermediate relations in words and check their schemas before composing a long expression.

自编练习：πA(σB>0(R))能否变成σB>0(πA(R))？

Can πA(σB>0(R)) become σB>0(πA(R))?

先独立尝试：写出条件、方法和理由，再展开。 / Try first: write the conditions, method and reasons before revealing the solution.

答案与推理 / Answer and reasoning

自编练习 / Author-created exercise

一般不能，右式先丢掉B，条件无从求值。安全的下推保留B：πA(σB>0(πA,B(R)))。优化不能以丢失后续需要的信息为代价。

Generally not: the right side removes B before evaluating the predicate. A safe push down retains it: πA(σB>0(πA, B(R))). Optimisation must preserve information needed by later operators.

C7-S2

自编练习：如何表达没有家属的员工？

How do you express employees who have no dependants?

先独立尝试：写出条件、方法和理由，再展开。 / Try first: write the conditions, method and reasons before revealing the solution.

答案与推理 / Answer and reasoning

自编练习 / Author-created exercise

先把EMPLOYEE.SSN与DEPENDENT.ESSN重命名为同名键，求所有员工键减有家属员工键，再连回EMPLOYEE投影姓名。不能用“连接后筛家属名不等于某值”代替不存在判断。

Rename EMPLOYEE.SSN and DEPENDENT.ESSN to a common key, subtract employees with dependants from all employee keys, then join back to names. A joined-row predicate saying a dependant name differs from some value does not express nonexistence.
