T12-E1-A

T1检查点后开始、崩溃前提交，需什么恢复？

T1 started after the checkpoint and committed before the crash. What recovery action is needed?

答案与推理 / Answer and reasoning

Redo。No-force使提交后的数据页可能仍未写盘，持久日志可重放AFIM保证持久性。并不是“开始在检查点后”本身导致Redo，而是提交与数据页持久状态。

Redo. No-force can leave committed pages un flushed; durable log AFIMs restore durability. Starting after the checkpoint alone is not the reason; commit status and page durability are.

答案与推理 / Answer and reasoning

T5在检查点已持久，无需重做。Redo提交T7的C=31与T6的Z=65，按原有效日志顺序；必须能追溯T7检查点前那条记录。忽略未提交T8(D13)和T9(X43)，因为延迟更新不让它们上盘，无需Undo。最终C31、Z65；D与X保留此前值，但题目未给旧值，不能编数字。课程“从检查点开始”描述会漏T7，需要上述补充。

T5 is already durable. Redo committed T7’s C=31 and T6’s Z=65 in effective log order, including T7’s pre-checkpoint record. Ignore uncommitted T8(D13)and T9(X43), since deferred update kept them off data storage and no Undo is required. Final C31, Z65; D/Xretain prior values, which are unspecified. Do not invent numbers. The course’s scan-from-checkpoint wording needs this correction.

T12-E2-immediate-extension

教学补充：若改为立即更新会怎样？

Teaching extension: what changes if immediate update is assumed instead?

先独立尝试：写出条件、方法和理由，再展开。 / Try first: write the conditions, method and reasons before revealing the solution.

答案与推理 / Answer and reasoning

课程提供答案；附逐步推导与更正 / Course answer with stepwise derivation and corrections

Steal/No-force时T8、T9可能已写盘，需Undo；但本日志没有D/X的BFIM，无法求具体恢复旧值。完整刷新检查点可能已持久化T7的C31，因此它的该项可无需Redo；T6仍需考虑Redo。必须一并改变日志需求和检查点语义，不能只改一个策略名称。

Under Steal/No-force, T8/T9 may have reached disk and need Undo, but the log lacks D/XBFIMsfor numerical restoration. Afull-flush checkpoint may already have made T7’s C31 durable, while T6 still needs Redo consideration. Change logging requirements and checkpoint semantics together, not just the policy name.
